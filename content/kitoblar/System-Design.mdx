# System Design

"Millionlab foydalanuvchiga xizmat qiladigan tizimni qanday quramiz?" degan savolga javob berish — ham intervyu mavzusi, ham real ko'nikma. Quyidagi manbalar fikrlash tarzini shakllantiradi va konkret pattern'larni o'rgatadi.

## Bepul manbalar

- [The System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) — Donne Martin; load balancing, caching, sharding, CAP — hammasi diagrammalar bilan bir joyda, butunlay bepul, boshlash uchun ideal.
- [ByteByteGo](https://bytebytego.com/) va [YouTube kanali](https://www.youtube.com/@ByteByteGo) — Alex Xu; murakkab tizimlarni sodda vizual animatsiyalar bilan tushuntiradi, kunlik intuitsiya uchun.
- [Grokking the System Design Interview](https://www.designgurus.io/course/grokking-the-system-design-interview) — DesignGurus; masalalarni bosqichma-bosqich yechish shablonini beradigan mashhur kurs.

## Kitoblar

- [System Design Interview, Vol. 1](https://www.amazon.com/System-Design-Interview-insiders-Second/dp/B08CMF2CQF) va [Vol. 2](https://www.amazon.com/System-Design-Interview-Insiders-Guide/dp/1736049119) — Alex Xu; har bir bob bitta real masalani boshdan-oyoq yechadigan eng amaliy intervyu kitoblari.
- [Web Scalability for Startup Engineers](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/web-scalability-for/9780071843652/) — Artur Ejsmont; intervyu emas, real loyihani millionlab foydalanuvchiga chidaydigan qilib o'stirish haqida.
