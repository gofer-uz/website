# Distributed Systems

Bir mashinadagi dastur bilan o'nlab mashinaga tarqalgan tizim o'rtasida yer bilan osmoncha farq bor: tarmoq ishonchsiz, vaqt bir xil emas, mashinalar to'satdan o'ladi. Go bu sohada eng ko'p ishlatiladigan tillardan biri (Kubernetes, etcd, CockroachDB), shuning uchun bu mavzu biz uchun juda dolzarb.

## Kitoblar

- [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) — Martin Kleppmann; bittagina kitob o'qisangiz shu — replikatsiya, partitioning, consistency va tranzaksiyalarni hayratlanarli aniqlik bilan tushuntiradi.
- [Understanding Distributed Systems](https://understandingdistributed.systems/) — Roberto Vitillo; DDIA og'irlik qilsa, xuddi shu mavzularni yengilroq beradigan zamonaviy kirish.
- [Database Internals](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/database-internals/9781492040330/) — Alex Petrov; storage engine'lar (B-tree, LSM-tree) va distributed database'lar ichida nima bo'layotganini ochadi.
- [Distributed Systems](https://www.distributed-systems.net/index.php/books/ds4/) — van Steen & Tanenbaum; muallif bepul tarqatadigan klassik akademik darslik, nazariy fundament uchun.

## Kurs va maqolalar

- [MIT 6.824 — Distributed Systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.824/) — dunyodagi eng yaxshi kurs, bepul video va laboratoriyalari **Go'da** (MapReduce va Raft'ni o'z qo'lingiz bilan yozasiz).
- [Raft paper](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf) — Ongaro & Ousterhout; consensus'ning eng tushunarli izohi, [raft.github.io](https://raft.github.io/) vizualizatsiyasi bilan birga o'qing.

---

Yo'nalish: Understanding Distributed Systems yoki DDIA bilan boshlang, Database Internals bilan chuqurlashing, MIT 6.824 va Raft bilan nazariyani Go kodiga aylantiring.
