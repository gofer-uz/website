# SRE — Site Reliability Engineering

SRE — Google'da tug'ilgan yondashuv: tizim ishonchliligini muhandislik masalasi sifatida ko'rish. "Qanchalik ishonchli bo'lishi kerak?", "qachon yangi funksiya to'xtatilib, barqarorlikka o'tiladi?" kabi savollarga aniq javob beradi. Go bu dunyoda infratuzilma va monitoring vositalarining asosiy tili.

## Bepul kitoblar (Google)

- [Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) — Google'ning asl SRE kitobi, to'liq bepul; SLO, error budget, on-call va incident'lar haqidagi fundament.
- [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) — birinchi kitobning amaliy davomi, "nazariyani qanday joriy qilamiz?" degan savolga real misollar bilan javob beradi.

## Kitoblar

- [Observability Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/observability-engineering/9781492076438/) — Charity Majors va boshqalar; logs/metrics/traces'dan nariga o'tib, tizimni haqiqatan "ko'rish" haqida zamonaviy asar.
- [Seeking SRE](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/seeking-sre/9781491978856/) — David Blank-Edelman (muharrir); turli kompaniyalar SRE'ni qanday amalga oshirgani haqida keng nuqtai nazarlar to'plami.

---

Yo'nalish: Google SRE book bilan tushunchalarni o'zlashtiring, Workbook bilan amaliyotga o'ting, Observability Engineering bilan tizimni kuzatishni chuqurlashing.
