# Dizayn patternlar

Dizayn pattern'lar — bir xil muammolarga jamiyat topgan sinovdan o'tgan yechimlar. Ularni yodlab, hamma joyga tiqishtirmang; qachon *kerak emasligini* bilish ham xuddi shunchalik muhim.

Saytimizda pattern'larni Go misollari bilan ko'rsatadigan to'liq [Dizayn Patternlar](/dizayn-patternlar) bo'limi bor — amaliyot uchun avval o'sha yerga qarang. Bu sahifa esa tashqi manbalar ro'yxati.

## Manbalar

- [Refactoring.Guru](https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns) — pattern'larni rasm, misol va tushunarli til bilan ochadigan eng yaxshi bepul onlayn manba, boshlash uchun ideal.
- [Head First Design Patterns](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/head-first-design/9781492077992/) — Freeman & Robson; pattern'larni birinchi marta o'rganish uchun eng do'stona, vizual va yodda qoladigan kitob.
- [Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/design-patterns-elements/0201633612/) — "Gang of Four"; mavzuni boshlab bergan asl klassika, zich va ma'lumotnoma sifatida.

---

Yo'nalish: Refactoring.Guru yoki Head First bilan tanishing, GoF'ni ma'lumotnoma sifatida saqlang, keyin har birini Go'da [Dizayn Patternlar](/dizayn-patternlar) bo'limidagi misollar orqali mustahkamlang.
