# Go kitoblari va manbalari

Go'ni o'rganish uchun kerak bo'lgan narsalarning ko'pi bepul va onlayn. Manbalar ko'p, sifat har xil — quyida biz haqiqatan foyda ko'rganlari.

## Bepul onlayn (shu yerdan boshlang)

- [A Tour of Go](https://go.dev/tour/) — brauzerda kod yozib o'rganadigan rasmiy interaktiv darslik; tilni birinchi marta ushlab ko'rish uchun.
- [Go by Example](https://gobyexample.com/) — har bir mavzu bitta ishlaydigan misol orqali; sintaksisni eslay olmaganda ochadigan ma'lumotnoma.
- [Effective Go](https://go.dev/doc/effective_go) — "idiomatik" Go kod nima ekanini tushuntiruvchi rasmiy hujjat, sintaksisdan keyin o'qing.
- [Go rasmiy hujjatlari](https://go.dev/doc/) va [pkg.go.dev](https://pkg.go.dev/) — standart kutubxonani o'rganishning yagona to'g'ri manbasi.

## Kitoblar

- [The Go Programming Language](https://www.gopl.io/) — Donovan & Kernighan; Go'ning "K&R"i, tilni chuqur o'rganish uchun bitta klassik kitob.
- [Learning Go](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/learning-go-2nd/9781098139285/) — Jon Bodner; bugun Go'ni noldan o'rganish uchun eng yaxshi zamonaviy kitob, idiomatik uslubni birinchi kundan o'rgatadi.
- [100 Go Mistakes and How to Avoid Them](https://www.manning.com/books/100-go-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them) — Teiva Harsanyi; kodingizni "ishlaydi"dan "to'g'ri"ga o'tkazadigan, real xatolar to'plami.
- [Concurrency in Go](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/concurrency-in-go/9781491941294/) — Katherine Cox-Buday; goroutine va channel'larni *nega* va *qachon* ishlatishni o'rgatadigan yagona to'liq manba.
- [Let's Go](https://lets-go.alexedwards.net/) va [Let's Go Further](https://lets-go-further.alexedwards.net/) — Alex Edwards; standart kutubxona bilan real web ilova va production API quradigan amaliy loyiha darsliklari.

---

Yo'nalish: Learning Go yoki The Go Programming Language bilan boshlang, 100 Go Mistakes bilan sayqal bering, Concurrency in Go bilan chuqurlashing, Let's Go bilan amaliyot qiling.
