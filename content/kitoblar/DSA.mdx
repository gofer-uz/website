# Ma'lumotlar tuzilmalari va algoritmlar

Algoritmlar shunchaki intervyu uchun emas — ular "qaysi yechim tez, qaysi biri xotira yeydi" degan hissni beradi. Quyida fundament (kitoblar) va amaliyot (platformalar) uchun manbalar.

Saytimizda algoritmlar bo'yicha alohida [DSA](/dsa) bo'limi ham bor — Go'dagi tuzilmalar va algoritmlarni amalda ko'rish uchun avval o'sha yerga qarang, bu sahifa esa tashqi manbalar to'plami.

## Amaliyot (avval shulardan)

- [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/) — mavzu bo'yicha guruhlangan masalalar (NeetCode 150) va tushunarli video yechimlar; tayyorgarlikning eng samarali yo'li.
- [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) — masalalar ombori va intervyu tayyorgarligining de-fakto standarti; NeetCode ro'yxati bo'ylab tanlab yeching.

## Kitoblar

- [Grokking Algorithms](https://www.manning.com/books/grokking-algorithms) — Aditya Bhargava; rasmlar bilan, matematik og'irliksiz eng do'stona kirish, boshlash uchun ideal.
- [The Algorithm Design Manual](https://www.algorist.com/) — Steven Skiena; nazariya va amaliyot muvozanati, "war stories" bilan tirik; ko'p muhandislarning sevimlisi.
- [Algorithms](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/home/) — Sedgewick & Wayne; sof va tizimli, bepul [Coursera kursi](https://www.coursera.org/learn/algorithms-part1) bilan.
- [Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262046305/introduction-to-algorithms/) — Cormen va boshqalar; "muqaddas kitob" va ma'lumotnoma, boshdan-oyoq emas — kerak bo'lganda ochiladi.

## Go bilan

- [TheAlgorithms/Go](https://github.com/TheAlgorithms/Go) — klassik algoritmlarning Go'dagi ochiq kodli to'plami; kitoblardagi misollarni Go'da qayta yozib mustahkamlang.

---

Yo'nalish: Grokking Algorithms bilan qo'rquvni yenging, NeetCode 150 bilan mashq qiling, Skiena bilan chuqurlashing, CLRS'ni ma'lumotnoma sifatida saqlang.
