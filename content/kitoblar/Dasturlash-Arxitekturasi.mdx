# Dasturlash arxitekturasi

Katta tizimni bo'laklarga bo'lish, qaysi qism qayerda turishi va yillar davomida qanday o'sishini hal qilish — kod yozishdan boshqa mahorat. Quyida dizayn prinsiplari va arxitektura bo'yicha manbalar.

## Prinsiplar va fundament

- [Clean Architecture](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/clean-architecture-a/9780134494272/) — Robert C. Martin; dependency'lar qaysi tomonga qarashi va biznes-mantiqni framework'dan ajratish haqidagi klassika (tanqidiy o'qing).
- [Fundamentals of Software Architecture](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/fundamentals-of-software/9781492043447/) — Richards & Ford; zamonaviy, muvozanatli, trade-off'larni baholashni o'rgatadigan eng yaxshi kirish.
- [Software Architecture: The Hard Parts](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/software-architecture-the/9781492086888/) — Ford, Richards va boshqalar; aniq javobi yo'q qiyin savollarga halol yondashadigan "og'ir" davom, tajribaliroqlar uchun.

## Domain va servis'lar

- [Domain-Driven Design](https://www.domainlanguage.com/ddd/) — Eric Evans; murakkab biznes-mantiqni kodga tarjima qilish va umumiy til qurish haqidagi "ko'k kitob".
- [Building Microservices](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-microservices-2nd/9781492034018/) — Sam Newman; mikroservislar qachon kerak, qachon keraksizligini halol aytadigan aqlli kitob (Go jamoasi uchun dolzarb).
- [Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture](https://martinfowler.com/books/eaa.html) — Martin Fowler; Repository, Unit of Work, Data Mapper kabi bugun ham yashaydigan pattern'lar ma'lumotnomasi.

---

Yo'nalish: Fundamentals of Software Architecture bilan umumiy manzarani ko'ring, Building Microservices bilan servis'lar dunyosiga kiring, DDD bilan murakkab domenlarni modellang.
